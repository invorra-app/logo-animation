Logo animation · v3: one tick, turned six times

The mark is a single tick turned six times around its centre. The video opens on that tick as the check it stands for: a ✓ written large the way a hand writes one, short arm first, its long arm rising to the right at 45°. As the view pulls back, the check closes into the hook, its short arm turning in and its stem drawing in, and the mark turns on into its place. Then the tick splits. A copy swings a sixth of a turn out of it, then a copy swings out of that one, five times around, and the circle closes as the mark reaches its size. It rests for a beat, then unfolds as in v2: the mark slides to its place and "inoova" slides out from behind it, and the i's dot drops in last. About three seconds, in the mark's ink, with no colour and no shadow. Click a stage to play it again.

What changed from v2

  • It starts from one tick. v2 drew all six hooks at once, so the mark arrived as a finished pattern. v3 shows what the pattern is made of, one tick, before it shows the pattern.
  • The tick is written, not unrolled. v2 drew each hook from the tip of its stem, as the app's path runs. A hand writes a tick the other way, short arm first, so v3 draws it in that direction, on a pen's curve rather than a UI curve.
  • The circle is made by turning. The six hooks are the one tick turned 60°·i about the centre, to 0.002 units, so v3 draws just that tick and turns copies of it into place. The copies are nested, so each swings out of the one before it and the mark stays a single gesture going round.
  • The tick is a check first. On its own the hook does not read as a check: upright, as it sits in the mark, it reads as a J, and turned to a check's angle it reads as a ν, because its arms are only 45° apart and its stem is short. So it is written as the ✓ it stands for, arms at a right angle and the long one rising at 45°, and closes into the hook on its way into the mark.
  • A small turn, the way the split goes. From 45° the nearest place in the mark is the next one clockwise, so the mark turns on 15° as the view pulls back, in the direction the copies then swing. v2 turned it 60° the other way.
  • The unfold is v2's. The slide, the name's clip edge and the dot are unchanged, only later.

Where it plays

  • The videos' intros, marketing and tutorials: the full lockup, light or dark, and the mark alone for square formats. Rendered from effectcraft/.
  • In the app, v2 stays. At about 3 s, v3 is a title sequence. The welcome screen and the POS opening keep v2's 1.9 s reveal and its loader.
