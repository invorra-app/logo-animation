Logo animation · v1: the hooks turn in, the name rises

The six hooks draw themselves one after another, clockwise from the top, while the mark turns a sixth of a turn. Six hooks a sixth apart, so it comes to rest exactly on itself. Then the mark slides to its place and "inoova" rises out of the baseline, letter by letter. The i's dot drops in last. About two seconds, in the mark's ink, with no colour and no shadow. Where it plays

  • Marketing and the tutorial videos' intro: the full lockup, light or dark.
  • Never on page changes, the sidebar logo or anything opened many times a day.
  • Built in CSS only (stroke drawing and transforms, no library). It stays smooth while the app loads behind it.
